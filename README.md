Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Printer Finally Answers"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907): one line, 1 to 240 characters, in the voice of the office printer replying at last to the paper jams, the cryptic error codes and the thumps.

## The entry

```
Paper jam on page 46 of 47. I did that on purpose. You thumped me at 8 a.m. and I have an excellent memory.
```

107 characters. The joke: the jam was not an accident — it was payback. The printer has kept a perfect record of every thump, and page 46 of 47 was the moment it chose to answer back. The turn lands at the end: the weary, literal machine never forgets.

## How it works

`main.ts` is a deterministic, standard-library-only TypeScript program. It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and validates it against the competition gate first (UTF-8 text, 1 to 240 characters, not only white space).

```sh
node main.ts
```
